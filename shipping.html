Where we deliver (serviceable PIN codes)

We deliver to PIN codes across India that our courier partners serve. The checkout only accepts a valid Indian PIN code that matches the state you choose. Before dispatch we confirm the courier can deliver to your PIN code; if none can, we tell you and refund you in full. We don't ship outside India.

Couriers

We ship with reputed courier partners such as Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC, Ekart, Xpressbees, Shadowfax and India Post, choosing the best option for your PIN code. The courier and tracking number are in your dispatch email and your account.

Cost

Delivery within India is free; it is included in the price.

Dispatch time

  • Every unit is tested and packed before it leaves us. In-stock orders are dispatched within 3 working days of payment.
  • Pre-booked products are dispatched when ready; we email the expected date and keep you updated.

Delivery time

Usually 3 to 7 working days after dispatch, depending on the PIN code. Remote areas, the North-East, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and the islands can take longer. These are estimates; we tell you if something is delayed.

Receiving your parcel

  • We ship to the delivery address chosen at checkout. Make sure the name, phone number and PIN code are right: the courier calls that number and may ask for an OTP.
  • If the box is visibly damaged or tampered with, you may refuse it or note it with the courier.
  • Please record a short video while opening the parcel. If anything is damaged or missing, email us within 48 hours of delivery with the order number, photos and the video.
  • If delivery fails after the courier's attempts (wrong address, not reachable), the parcel comes back to us and we contact you to ship it again; a second delivery may be charged at cost.

When risk passes to you

Ownership of the goods, and the risk of loss or damage, pass to you when the parcel is delivered to the delivery address. Until then, loss or damage in transit is our responsibility.
