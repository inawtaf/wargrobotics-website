Shipping and delivery

Free delivery anywhere in India, tested and packed before it leaves us, with tracking by email.

Where we deliver

Anywhere in India that our courier partners reach. We don't ship outside India yet. If we can't deliver to your pincode, we tell you and refund you in full.

Cost

Delivery within India is free: it is included in the price.

When it ships

  • Every unit is tested and packed before it leaves us. In-stock orders are usually dispatched within 3 working days of payment.
  • Pre-booked products ship when they are ready; we email the expected date and keep you updated.
  • When your order ships we email you the courier, tracking number and expected delivery date. You can follow it from your account too.

How long delivery takes

Usually 3 to 7 working days after dispatch, depending on your location. Remote areas, the North-East, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh and the islands can take longer. These are estimates, not guarantees; we'll tell you if something is delayed.

Receiving your parcel

  • We ship to the delivery address you choose at checkout. Make sure the name, phone number and pincode are right: the courier calls that number.
  • Check the parcel when it arrives. If the box is visibly damaged or tampered with, you may refuse it or note it with the courier.
  • Please record a short video while opening the parcel. If anything is damaged or missing, email us within 48 hours of delivery with the order number, photos and the video.
  • If delivery fails after the courier's attempts (wrong address, not reachable), the parcel comes back to us. We'll contact you to ship it again; a second delivery may be charged at cost.

Ownership and risk

The goods are your property, and at your risk, once they are delivered.

Questions
