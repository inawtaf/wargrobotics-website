    What happens next

    1. We read it and reply

      Target is one working day. If what you describe is a setting or a known fix, the reply is the whole answer.

    2. We confirm the fault

      Usually from your description and the photographs. Sometimes we will ask for a short clip of the behaviour.

    3. Repair, replacement or a fix in the app

      If the unit has to come back, we arrange that with you against the pincode you gave us.2
