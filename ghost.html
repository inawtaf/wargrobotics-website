Ghost Pro

The same camera and the same link, with a Linux processor in the transmitter body. Add it when you pre-book.

Your code, on the aircraft

A standard Ghost sends a picture and records it. A Pro does that and runs your own software beside it, reading the full-rate frame where it is captured — no companion computer, no second power feed, nothing else bolted to the airframe.

What it is built for

Image processing, autonomous navigation, path finding, and precision flight in close quarters.

Configured to the job

Memory at 2, 4 or 8 GB and storage at 32, 64 or 128 GB, chosen independently of the camera.

Priced on the configuration. Pro is quoted rather than listed, so the pre-book form asks what you want it to do and we size it with you. Full platform specification on request.
