Premium video transmission

Digital from the sensor to the screen, with no analogue stage anywhere in the path. Picture quality is what separates the units in the range; everything else about them is deliberately the same.

HD digital
No analogue stage, so no static, no rolling interference and no slow fade at the edge of range.
60 ms glass to glass
Measured end to end on the bench rather than summed from component datasheets.
Frame rate where it counts
720p at 90 fps on Ghost for fast flying, 1080p at 30 fps on Greywind for detail, with 720p90 available on it too.
Long range transmission
Up to 5 km between the air unit and the ground unit.

Ready to go

No soldering, no firmware rabbit hole, no forum thread at midnight. The module arrives bound and configured, and everything you change afterwards you change from your app.

Plug and play
One cable to power and four bolts to mount. Nothing to flash before you get a picture.
Five minute setup
Box to first frame in about five minutes, including binding the ground unit.
Mobile and web app
Channel, bitrate, recording and firmware from your phone. Updates arrive over the air.

Any screen with HDMI

Most systems tie you to one goggle, and the goggle often costs more than the link itself. The ground unit here plugs to whatever screen is already in front of you.

HDMI out
The ground unit drives any HDMI screen directly, with no converter in between.
No goggle lock-in
A TV, a monitor, a tablet, your phone, or goggles you already own. The choice stops being part of the purchase.

Record while you fly

The module records to its own storage at the same time as it streams to your screen. You are not choosing between seeing the flight and keeping it, and the recording is written on the air unit rather than reconstructed from the transmitted stream.

Live and record together
Both run at once. Link going down does not take the recording with it.
Onboard storage
8 GB on Ghost, 16 GB on Greywind. No card to buy, lose or forget.
Straight off over USB
Plug the air unit into a computer and pull the files. No proprietary reader and no app in the middle.

Fits what you already fly

Light and small enough for a 2 inch freestyle quad, and indifferent to what it is bolted to. The mount pattern is universal, so the module goes onto the drone you already own, no need to buy a new one.

All sizes
Small enough for a 2 inch freestyle build, unchanged on anything larger.
All wing types
Quads, fixed wing and VTOL. Ground vehicles and fixed surveillance rigs take the same module.
Retrofit or new build
Upgrade an existing drone without replacing it, or design the module into a build from the start.

Where the sixty milliseconds goes

Five stages, in series, from the light hitting the sensor to the picture on your screen.

60 ms end to end, measured on the bench rather than summed from component datasheets.

What can it do

Three jobs the module is doing today, with what we have proven on each and what is still ahead of us.

In production

FPV and freestyle

The original case, and still the hardest. Below about a hundred milliseconds the link stops being something a pilot compensates for, which is the entire point.

Proven

  • Up to 60 ms sustained through gates and tight lines
  • Link holds under hard banking and prop wash

In pipeline

  • Channel diversity and frequency hopping
  • 4k recording
  • Head-tracking and IO integration
Field trials

Computer vision

Opens up autonomous behaviour, not just assisted piloting. For anything reactive (obstacle avoidance, target tracking, gimbal/gesture control), round-trip latency is often the difference between "works" and "oscillates or crashes."

Proven

  • Stable link at extended standoff
  • Onboard compute powered by Warg SDK

In pipeline

  • Thermal and multispectral camera support
  • NVIDIA Jetson compatibility
Field trials

Remote cockpit

Range and encryption matter more than latency here. Assets sit a long way from an operator, and the footage is often evidence, so the transport has to be defensible as well as clear.

Proven

  • Survives sustained chassis vibration
  • Low mounting height

In pipeline

  • Gimbal compatibility
  • Headtracking support
  • Long range support up to 25 km
