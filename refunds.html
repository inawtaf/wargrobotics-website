Cancelling an order

  • Before dispatch: cancel at any time for a full refund. Email hello@wargrobotics.com or raise a service request from your account with the order number. Pre-booked orders can be cancelled until they are dispatched.
  • After dispatch: the order can no longer be cancelled, but you can refuse the delivery or return the item as below.
  • If we cancel an order (for example a product is unavailable, the PIN code isn't serviceable or a price was clearly wrong), you get a full refund automatically.

Returns: damaged, faulty or wrong items

Window: tell us within 7 days of delivery (transit damage within 48 hours, see our shipping policy). Conditions: include your order number and photos or a short video; keep all parts and the packaging. We arrange the pickup at our cost and send a replacement, or refund you in full if you prefer or a replacement isn't available.

Returns: changed your mind

Window: within 7 days of delivery. Conditions: the unit must be unopened, in its original sealed packaging; ask us first by email for the return address; you pay for the return shipping. We refund the full price of the item once it reaches us unopened. Opened units can't be returned for a change of mind; faults are covered by our warranty.

Refunds

  • To the original payment method: refunds always go back to the account, card or UPI ID you paid from, through Razorpay. We don't refund in cash or to a different account.
  • Timeline: we start the refund within 2 working days of the cancellation, or of receiving and checking a returned item. Your bank then usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it; UPI is often faster.
  • Failed payments: if money left your account but the payment failed, Razorpay returns it automatically, usually within 5 to 7 working days. No order is created.
  • For a GST invoice, we issue a credit note for the refunded amount.
  • Offers: a refund covers what you actually paid. If you return one item of a combo pair, the refund is the amount paid for the pair minus the full price of the item you keep. A WARG20 coupon used on a cancelled or refunded order is not reissued.

Questions and complaints

Email hello@wargrobotics.com. If you are unhappy with how a return or refund was handled, contact our Grievance Officer at grievance@wargrobotics.com or +91 86689 23286; we acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within one month.
