Refunds and cancellation

Cancel before dispatch for a full refund. Damaged, faulty or wrong items are replaced or refunded.

Cancelling an order

  • Before dispatch: cancel any time, for a full refund. Email hello@wargrobotics.com or raise a service request from your account with the order number. Pre-booked orders can be cancelled until they are dispatched.
  • After dispatch: the order can no longer be cancelled, but you can refuse the delivery or return it as below.
  • We may cancel an order ourselves (for example if a product is unavailable or a price was clearly wrong). You then get a full refund automatically.

Returns: damaged, faulty or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, does not work out of the box, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos or a short video. We arrange the pickup at our cost and send a replacement, or refund you in full if you prefer or a replacement isn't available. Transit damage should be reported within 48 hours (see our shipping policy).

Returns: changed your mind

You can return an unopened unit in its original sealed packaging within 7 days of delivery. Ask first by email so we can give you the return address. You pay for the return shipping; we refund the full price of the item once it reaches us unopened. Opened units can't be returned for a change of mind; if something is wrong with one, the warranty in our terms of sale covers it.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the account or card you paid from, through Razorpay. We start the refund within 2 working days of the cancellation, or of receiving the returned item and checking it.
  • Your bank then usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it. UPI refunds are often faster.
  • For a GST invoice, we issue a credit note for the refunded amount.
  • Offers: a refund covers what you actually paid. If you return one item of a combo pair, the refund is the price paid for the pair minus the full price of the item you keep. A WARG20 coupon used on a cancelled or refunded order is not reissued.

Questions

Email hello@wargrobotics.com. If you are unhappy with how a return or refund was handled, our grievance officer is listed in the terms of sale.
