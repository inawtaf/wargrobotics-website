Privacy policy

What we collect when you use wargrobotics.com, why, who helps us process it, and how to see, correct or delete it.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Seller. WARG ROBOTICS PVT LTD ("Warg Robotics", "we", "us"). Registered address: 7th Floor, Opp. Shram Shakti Bhawan, Patto Road, Panaji, Goa 403001, India. GSTIN 30AAECW5751D1ZN. Email hello@wargrobotics.com.

Grievance Officer: +91 86689 23286, hello@wargrobotics.com. Complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within one month.

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use wargrobotics.com, why, who we share it with, how we protect it and your rights. It is written to meet the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 ("SPDI Rules"), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") as its provisions come into force. Under the DPDP Act we are the "data fiduciary" for this data.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, optional phone number, and your password, which we store only as a salted, one-way hash. We record when you confirmed your email.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your name, email address, whether it is verified, and a Google account ID. We don't get your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Addresses: delivery and billing addresses you save or enter at checkout, with phone numbers, and a business name and GSTIN if you add them.
  • Orders: what you bought, prices, discounts and coupons, GST, delivery status and tracking, invoices, and the emails we sent you about the order.
  • Payments: Razorpay processes your payment. We receive the payment ID, amount and status. We never receive or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs, net-banking passwords or other financial account details.
  • Cart: what is in your cart, and when you last changed it.
  • Service requests and forms: what you write to us through your account, or the contact and support forms (including photos you attach).
  • Technical data: IP address and browser details, used to keep accounts secure (for example limiting repeated sign-in attempts) and in short-lived server logs.
  • Analytics: pages viewed and buttons clicked, through Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics. Nothing you type into a form is sent to analytics.

Sensitive personal data (SPDI Rules)

Of the categories the SPDI Rules treat as sensitive, we hold only your password, and only as a one-way hash that no one, including us, can read back. We do not collect financial account, card or payment-instrument details (Razorpay handles those), or health, biometric or similar data. By creating an account you consent to us storing your password this way.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, take payment, deliver your order, issue GST invoices and provide warranty service.
  • To send emails you need: email confirmation, password reset, order receipt and invoice, dispatch and delivery updates, and replies to your requests. We don't send marketing emails without your consent.
  • To see which customers have items waiting in their cart, so we can improve the shop and help if something is stopping you from ordering.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent fraud, and meet legal duties such as GST record-keeping.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used, and improve it.

We process your data with your consent (account, Google sign-in, forms, analytics cookies) and for the legitimate uses the law allows, such as fulfilling an order you placed and meeting tax law. We collect only what these purposes need.

Who we share it with

Only service providers who process data for us, under contract and with reasonable security practices, and only what each needs:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and the database that stores accounts and orders, and provides cookieless visitor statistics.
  • Razorpay: payments.
  • Zoho Books (Zoho Corporation): GST invoices and accounting.
  • Resend: sends our emails.
  • Google: "Continue with Google" sign-in, Google Analytics, and Google Workspace (form submissions and our email).
  • Courier partners: your name, delivery address and phone number, to deliver the parcel.

We also disclose data when the law requires it, for example to tax authorities or under a lawful order. We don't sell your data. Some providers process data outside India; we only use providers who protect it to standards at least as strong as Indian law requires.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • wr_session: keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days. Essential.
  • wr_oauth: used for 10 minutes during Google sign-in only. Essential.
  • Your cart, while signed out, is kept in your browser's local storage until you sign in.
  • Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga and similar) to count visits. You can block them in your browser; the shop works without them.

How long we keep it

  • Account and saved addresses: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Orders and invoices: as long as GST and accounting law requires (currently at least six years after the relevant year), even after account deletion.
  • Sign-in sessions, email links and security logs: days to weeks, then deleted automatically.

Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Warg account and personal data at any time. Email hello@wargrobotics.com with the subject "Delete my Warg account", from the email address on your account.

  • What we delete: your account and sign-in (password and Google link), saved addresses, cart, sessions, and service requests and form messages no longer needed.
  • What we must keep: orders and GST invoices, for as long as tax and accounting law requires, no longer linked to an account you can sign in to.
  • Open orders: finished or cancelled first.
  • When: we confirm by email within 30 days of your request.

Your rights

You can ask to access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we've shared it with, correct or update it, have it erased (except what the law makes us keep), and withdraw consent at any time (withdrawing doesn't affect processing already done, and some services, like your account, need it). You can nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to. You can update your name, phone and addresses yourself in your account; for anything else email hello@wargrobotics.com from your account's email address. We reply within 30 days.

Grievance Officer

For any concern about your data or how we handle it (SPDI Rules and DPDP Act), contact our Grievance Officer at hello@wargrobotics.com or +91 86689 23286. We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within one month. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is in operation.

Security

We follow reasonable security practices: the site is served only over HTTPS; passwords are hashed; sign-in cookies can't be read by scripts; sign-in attempts are rate-limited; and access to order data is limited to staff who need it. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Our shop is not meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect children's data; if you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top; for important changes we'll also tell account holders by email.
