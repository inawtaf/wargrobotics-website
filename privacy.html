Privacy policy

What we collect when you use wargrobotics.com, why, who helps us process it, and how to see, correct or delete it.

Last updated 31 October 2026

Who we are. wargrobotics.com is run by Warg Robotics Private Limited. ("Warg Robotics", "we", "us"), 7th Floor, Opp. Shram Shakti Bhawan, Patto Road, Panaji, Goa 403001, India. GSTIN 30AAECW5751D1ZN. Email hello@wargrobotics.com.

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use wargrobotics.com, why, who we share it with and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We are the "data fiduciary" for this data.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, optional phone number, and your password, which we store only as a salted, one-way hash. We record when you confirmed your email.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us your name, email address, whether it is verified, and a Google account ID. We don't get your Google password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Addresses: delivery and billing addresses you save or enter at checkout, with phone numbers, and a business name and GSTIN if you add them.
  • Orders: what you bought, prices, discounts and coupons, GST, delivery status and tracking, invoices, and the emails we sent you about the order.
  • Payments: Razorpay processes your payment. We receive the payment ID, amount and status; we never receive or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or bank passwords.
  • Cart: what is in your cart, and when you last changed it.
  • Service requests and forms: what you write to us through your account, or the contact, support and pre-booking forms (including any photos you attach).
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser details, used to keep accounts secure (for example limiting repeated sign-in attempts) and in short-lived server logs.
  • Analytics: pages viewed and buttons clicked, through Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics. Nothing you type into a form is sent to analytics.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, take payment, deliver your order, issue GST invoices and provide warranty service.
  • To send emails you need: email confirmation, password reset, order confirmation and invoice, dispatch and delivery updates, and replies to your requests. We don't send marketing emails without your consent.
  • To see which of our customers have items waiting in their cart, so we can improve the shop and, where you have an account, help if something is stopping you from ordering.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent fraud and meet our legal duties, including GST record-keeping.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used, and improve it.

We use your data because you asked us to (to create an account or buy something), because you consented (Google sign-in, analytics cookies, forms), or because the law requires it (tax records).

Who we share it with

Only service providers who process data for us, under contract, and only what each needs:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and the database that stores accounts and orders, and provides cookieless visitor statistics.
  • Razorpay: payments.
  • Zoho Books (Zoho Corporation): GST invoices and accounting, with your name, billing and delivery address, GSTIN and order.
  • Resend: sends our emails.
  • Google: "Continue with Google" sign-in, Google Analytics, and Google Workspace, where form submissions and our email are kept.
  • Courier partners: your name, delivery address and phone number, to deliver the parcel.

We also share data when the law requires it, for example with tax authorities. We don't sell your data. Some of these providers process data outside India; they protect it under their own security standards and agreements with us.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • wr_session: keeps you signed in, for up to 30 days. Essential.
  • wr_oauth: used for 10 minutes during Google sign-in only. Essential.
  • Your cart, while you are signed out, is kept in your browser's local storage until you sign in.
  • Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga and similar) to count visits. You can block these with your browser settings or an ad blocker; the shop works without them.

How long we keep it

  • Account and saved addresses: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Orders and invoices: for as long as GST and accounting law requires (currently at least six years after the relevant year), even if you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions, email links and security logs: days to weeks, then deleted automatically.

Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Warg account and your personal data at any time. Email hello@wargrobotics.com with the subject "Delete my Warg account", from the email address on your account (so we know the request is really yours).

  • What we delete: your account and sign-in (password and Google link), saved addresses, cart, sessions, and service requests and form messages that are no longer needed.
  • What we must keep: orders and GST invoices, for as long as tax and accounting law requires. We keep them only for that purpose, no longer linked to an account you can sign in to.
  • Open orders: if an order hasn't been delivered yet, we finish or cancel it first, then delete the account.
  • When: we confirm by email once it's done, within 30 days of your request. After that you can't sign in, and anything not kept for the law can't be recovered.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (except what the law makes us keep), and you can withdraw consent at any time. You can update your name, phone and addresses yourself in your account. For anything else, email hello@wargrobotics.com from the email address on your account; to delete your account, see above. We reply within 30 days. You can also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, contact our grievance officer, Sam Singh, at grievance@wargrobotics.com or +91 86689 23286. You may then complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, sign-in cookies can't be read by scripts on the page, and access to order data is limited to our staff who need it.

Children

Our shop is not meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect children's data; if you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top; for important changes we'll also tell account holders by email.
