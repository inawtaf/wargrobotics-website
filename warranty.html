Period

Every Warg Ghost and Warg Greywind unit is covered for 12 months from the date of delivery against defects in materials and workmanship. A repaired or replacement unit is covered for the rest of the original period, or 90 days, whichever is longer.

What we do

If a covered fault appears, we repair the unit or replace it with the same or an equivalent model. If neither is possible, we refund the price you paid. We cover the courier costs both ways for valid claims within India.

What is not covered

  • Crash, impact or fall damage, and damage from propellers.
  • Water, moisture or corrosion damage.
  • Modification: units that have been opened, had their transmit power, antennas or firmware altered, or run unofficial firmware.
  • Unauthorised repair by anyone other than Warg Robotics or a service partner we name.
  • Damage from incorrect wiring, voltage, polarity or power supplies.
  • Normal wear, scratches and cosmetic damage; cables and accessories damaged in use.
  • Loss, theft, or loss of a drone, footage or data.
  • Use outside the operating conditions in the manual, or in breach of our regulatory use terms.

How to raise a claim (return-merchandise process)

  1. Raise a request: from your account (Orders → Request service), or through our service page, with your order number, a description of the fault and, if you can, a short video.
  2. We diagnose: within one working day we reply, often with a settings fix. If the unit needs to come in, we give you a return authorisation (RMA) number and arrange a courier pickup.
  3. Pack it: in the original packaging if you have it, with the RMA number on the parcel. Remove propellers and batteries.
  4. We repair or replace: usually within 7 working days of receiving the unit, and ship it back to you with tracking. If the fault isn't covered, we tell you first and quote for the repair; nothing is charged without your agreement.

Your rights

This warranty is in addition to, and does not affect, your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. Questions or complaints: hello@wargrobotics.com, or our Grievance Officer at grievance@wargrobotics.com / +91 86689 23286.
